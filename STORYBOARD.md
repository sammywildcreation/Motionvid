## Video direction

- Palette from the designs: Grasp orange #FA5D19, ink #14100E, warm canvas #F6F3EF, white.
- Type: Cal Sans for display words, Onest for lines (both used in the Figma file).
- Screens shown as rounded cards with soft shadow, never redrawn; zooms are camera moves on the PNG.
- Motion: expo/power4 punches on the beat, slow drift while holding, hard cuts.
